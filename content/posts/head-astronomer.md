description: "When his arms embraced her frame"

When I saw the head Astronomer
When his arms embraced her frame
When the jumbotron showed their rapturous repose
When I saw the astronomer duck his head amongst the jeering of the crowd
How soon unaccountable I became tired and sick
Till rising and gliding out, I checked myself
In my dark room in the glow of the screen
Cued up cat videos and smiled
