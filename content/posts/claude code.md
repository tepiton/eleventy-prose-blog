You see that little light/dark switcher on the upper right corner of this site? I had Claude Code do that for me. I've implemented a light/dark switch before, but Claude was able to do this in less than an hour.

Which got me thinking.

Once in a while I get an idea for a site, and I'll buy a domain for it.[^5] Or I just come up with a euphonious word.[^1]  A while back I bought the domain `gravitique.com` because, well  "gravity" and "boutique". How could you not?

The problem is that, while I have good ideas,[^2] I'm not very talented.[^3] But then I think of   [Jeff Koons](../pages/Jeff%20Koons.md) and the balloon dogs.

I don't need talent. I need an amanuensis to implement my vision.

[Claude Code](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-code) is the new hotness, so I gave it a try with `gravitique`. I had a vague idea of a boutique that did business in gravity-related things. Let's see what Claude did with it:

> Here we are going to create a modern landing page. We're starting from scratch.
> 
> The website should have a mid-century modern feel. The name of the website is gravitique.com. It sells technology that leverages gravity for the one percent. Whatever. The thing is to have a nice landing page and we'll go from there. You game?

It quickly created the files. I didn't give it any direction on what the page should look like. You can see from the result that it got the landing page style right.

The end result: [Gravitique - Gravity Technology for the Elite](https://gravitique.netlify.app/)

It came up with this all by itself. I had no idea that Gravitique offered spatial manipulation technology.

> #### SPATIAL DYNAMICS
>
> Transform your environment with revolutionary spatial manipulation technology.

What's interesting is how Claude had an idea[^4] of the kind of site I wanted. I don't know that it caught the sarcasm, but then my intent was on making it look like a straight site.

Next up: Does Claude have a sense of humor, and if so, whose?
