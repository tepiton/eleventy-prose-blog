The iPhone front-facing camera preview always shows a mirrored view. You can't change this. If you're using the front-facing camera, the preview is like looking in a mirror. 

You can choose whether the resulting image is mirrored (so you look like what you saw) or not. 

I just learned this, but there's an aftertaste of memory, which makes me think I might have known this once.
