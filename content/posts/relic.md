title: Relic
---

With Tonka trucks we dig the sand
in search of buried treasure.
I bend with him against the sun
and excavate the relics of our love.

His treasures come up one by one,
eagerly sought and easily found:
a button is a gold doubloon
a stone the tooth of a dimetridon
a flake of mica a dragon's scale
a nail the sword that killed him.

My trucks shift sand from pile to pile.
I bend to him and kiss his hair.
It smells of air and summer sun
as yours did years ago
on the night that we conceived him.
